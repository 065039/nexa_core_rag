## Employee Handbook

Version 2026.1, effective 01 January 2026. Applies to all employees of NexaCore Technologies.

### 1. Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

### 2. Probation and Confirmation

New employees serve a probation period of 6 months. Confirmation is based on a performance review by the reporting manager.

### 3. Notice Period

| **Employee category**   | **Notice period**   |
|-------------------------|---------------------|
| During probation        | 15 days             |
| Confirmed employees     | 60 days             |
| Managers and above      | 90 days             |

### 4. Expense Claims

Employees should submit business expense claims within 45 days of incurring the expense. Please refer to the Finance Expense Reimbursement Policy for limits.

### 5. Grievance Redressal

An employee may raise a grievance with the HR Business Partner within 30 days of the incident. HR will acknowledge the grievance within 3 working days.

### 6. Code of Conduct

- Treat colleagues, customers and vendors with respect.
- Declare any conflict of interest to HR in writing.
- Gifts from vendors above INR 2,000 in value must be declined.
