## Leave Policy (Version 2025.1)

Effective date: 01 April 2025. Policy owner: Head of HR. This version is superseded by Leave Policy 2026.1.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the leave entitlements available to all permanent employees of NexaCore Technologies.

## 2. Leave Entitlements

| Leave type      | Entitlement      | Notes                                                |
|-----------------|------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Annual leave    | 18 days per year | Accrues monthly                                      |
| Sick leave      | 10 days per year | Medical certificate needed beyond 2 consecutive days |
| Maternity leave | 26 weeks         | As per Maternity Benefit Act                         |
| Paternity leave | 5 days           | To be availed within 3 months of birth               |

## 3. Carry Forward

Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 days of unused annual leave to the next calendar year. Any balance above 5 days lapses on 31 December.

## 4. Work From Home

Employees may work from home for up to 2 days per week with prior approval from their reporting manager.

## 5. Applying for Leave

- [ ] · Apply on the HRMS portal at least 7 days in advance for planned leave.

- [ ] · Unplanned leave must be informed to the manager before 10:00 AM on the same day.

- [ ] · Leave without approval is treated as unauthorised absence.
