## Leave Policy (Version 2026.1)

Effective date: 01 April 2026. Policy owner: Head of HR. This version replaces Leave Policy 2025.1.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the leave entitlements available to all permanent employees of NexaCore Technologies.

## 2. Leave Entitlements

| Leave type      | Entitlement      | Notes                                                |
|-----------------|------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Annual leave    | 20 days per year | Accrues monthly                                      |
| Sick leave      | 12 days per year | Medical certificate needed beyond 2 consecutive days |
| Maternity leave | 26 weeks         | As per Maternity Benefit Act                         |
| Paternity leave | 10 days          | To be availed within 6 months of birth               |

## 3. Carry Forward

Employees may carry forward a maximum of 10 days of unused annual leave to the next calendar year. Any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 December.

## 4. Work From Home

Employees may work from home for up to 3 days per week with prior approval from their reporting manager.

## 5. Applying for Leave

· Apply on the HRMS portal at least 7 days in advance for planned leave. · Unplanned leave must be informed to the manager before 10:00 AM on the same day. Leave without approval is treated as unauthorised absence.
