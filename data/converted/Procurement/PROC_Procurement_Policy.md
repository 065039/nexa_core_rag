## Procurement Policy

Version 2026.1, effective 01 January 2026. Owner: Head of Procurement.

1. Quotations

- [ ] At least three written quotations are required for any purchase above INR 1,00,000. Single-source purchases require a written justification approved by the Head of Procurement.

## 2. Purchase Orders

- [ ] A Purchase Order must be issued before any goods are delivered or work begins. Work started without a PO will not be paid.

## 3. Vendor Onboarding Process

New vendors are onboarded through the following steps, owned by different departments:

|   Step | Activity                                                             | Owner department   |
|--------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------|
|      1 | Business team raises a vendor request form on the procurement portal | Procurement        |
|      2 | Vendor KYC: PAN, GSTIN and certificate of incorporation              | Procurement        |
|      3 | Security assessment for vendors that access data or systems          | IT                 |
|      4 | NDA and Master Services Agreement signed                             | Legal              |
|      5 | Bank detail verification                                             | Finance            |
|      6 | Vendor code created in ERP                                           | Procurement        |

The complete onboarding process should be finished within 7 working days of receiving all documents.

## 4. Vendor Performance Review

Active vendors are reviewed annually using the Vendor Evaluation Matrix. Vendors scoring below 70 are placed on a performance improvement plan.
