Version 2026.1, effective 01 January 2026. Owner: General Counsel.

## 1. Contract Review

All contracts must be reviewed by the Legal team before signature. The standard review turnaround time is 5 business days.

## 2. Signing Authority

| Contract value (INR)     | Legal reviewer   | Authorised signatory   |
|--------------------------|------------------|------------------------|
| Up to 25,00,000          | Legal Counsel    | Department Head        |
| 25,00,001 to 1,00,00,000 | General Counsel  | CFO                    |
| Above 1,00,00,000        | General Counsel  | CEO                    |

## 3. Contract Retention

Signed contracts are stored in the contract repository and retained for 8 years after expiry or termination.

## 4. Renewals

Contract owners must start renewal discussions at least 60 days before contract expiry.
