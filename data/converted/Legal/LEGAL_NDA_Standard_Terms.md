## Non-Disclosure Agreement: Standard Terms

Version 2026.1. For use by Legal Counsel only. Deviations from these terms require General Counsel approval.

### 1. Type of NDA

NexaCore uses a mutual NDA template for vendors and partners. One-way NDAs are used only for candidate interviews involving confidential projects.

### 2. Confidentiality Period

Confidentiality obligations survive for 3 years after termination of the agreement. Trade secrets remain protected indefinitely.

### 3. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

The NDA is governed by the laws of India. Courts in New Delhi have exclusive jurisdiction.

### 4. Return of Information

On termination, the receiving party must return or destroy confidential information within 15 days and certify destruction in writing.
