## Data Privacy Policy

Version 2026.1, effective 01 March 2026. Owner: Data Protection Officer (DPO). Aligned with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

### 1. Personal Data Breach

Any personal data breach must be reported internally to the Data Protection Officer within 6 hours of discovery.

### 2. Data Principal Requests

Requests from customers to access, correct or erase their personal data must be completed within 30 days.

### 3. Retention

Customer personal data is retained for 3 years after account closure and then deleted or anonymised.

### 4. Consent

Marketing communication is sent only to customers who have given explicit opt-in consent.
