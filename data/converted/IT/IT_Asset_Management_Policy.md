## IT Asset Management Policy

Version 2026.1, effective 15 January 2026. Owner: IT Manager.

### 1. Laptop Allocation and Refresh

Every employee is issued one laptop. Laptops are refreshed every 4 years or earlier if declared beyond economical repair by IT.

### 2. Lost or Stolen Devices

A lost or stolen laptop must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours. IT will remotely wipe the device. An FIR copy is required for stolen devices.

### 3. Software Requests

Software requests are raised on the ServiceDesk portal and approved by the IT Manager. Approved requests are fulfilled within 3 business days.

### 4. Asset Return

Exiting employees must return all IT assets on or before their last working day. Unreturned assets are recovered from the full and final settlement.
