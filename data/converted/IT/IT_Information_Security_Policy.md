## Information Security Policy

Version 2026.1, effective 15 January 2026. Owner: Chief Information Security Officer (CISO).

## 1. Passwords and Authentication

- [ ] · Passwords must be at least 12 characters long.

- [ ] · Passwords must be changed every 90 days.

- [ ] · Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN and all cloud applications.

## 2. Device Security

All company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled. Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

## 3. Incident Reporting

Any suspected security incident, including phishing emails, must be reported to security@nexacore.example within 2 hours of detection.

## 4. Data Classification

| Class        | Examples               | Handling                                       |
|--------------|------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Public       | Marketing brochures    | No restriction                                 |
| Internal     | Policies, org charts   | Share inside NexaCore only                     |
| Confidential | Contracts, salary data | Need-to-know, encrypted in transit             |
| Restricted   | Customer personal data | Named access, encrypted at rest and in transit |

## 5. Acceptable Use

Personal USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops. Only software from the approved catalogue may be installed.
