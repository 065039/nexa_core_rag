## Customer Service Level Agreement

Version 2026.1, effective 01 January 2026. Applies to all enterprise customers of the NexaCore Cloud platform.

## 1. Response and Resolution Targets

| Priority   | Definition                  | First response   | Resolution target   |
|------------|-----------------------------|------------------|---------------------|
| P1         | Platform down for all users | 1 hour           | 8 hours             |
| P2         | Major feature unavailable   | 4 hours          | 24 hours            |
| P3         | Minor issue or question     | 1 business day   | 5 business days     |

## 2. Platform Availability

NexaCore commits to 99.5% monthly uptime, excluding scheduled maintenance announced 72 hours in advance.

## 3. Service Credits

If monthly uptime falls below 99.5%, the customer receives a service credit of 10% of the monthly fee. Below 98%, the credit is 25%.

## 4. Support Hours

P1 support is available 24x7. P2 and P3 support is available 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM IST on business days.
