## Complaint Handling and Refund Policy

Version 2026.1, effective 01 January 2026. Owner: Head of Customer Experience.

### 1. Acknowledgement

Every customer complaint must be acknowledged within 24 hours with a ticket number.

### 2. Escalation

- Level 1 support owns the complaint for the first 48 hours.
- Unresolved complaints move to Level 2 support after 48 hours.
- Complaints unresolved after 5 business days are escalated to the Head of Customer Experience.

### 3. Refunds

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the invoice date. Approved refunds are processed within 7 working days. Refunds above INR 50,000 require Finance Controller approval.

### 4. Complaint Closure

A complaint is closed only after the customer confirms resolution or does not respond for 7 days after the resolution is shared.
