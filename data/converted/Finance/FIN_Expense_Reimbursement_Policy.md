## Expense Reimbursement Policy

Version 2026.1, effective 01 January 2026. Policy owner: Chief Financial Officer.

## 1. Submission Timeline

All expense claims must be submitted on the Finance portal within 30 days of the date of expense. Claims submitted after 30 days will be rejected unless approved by the Finance Controller.

## 2. Receipts

Original receipts are mandatory for any single expense above INR 500.

## 3. Travel Limits

| Expense head                 | Metro cities   | Non-metro cities   |
|------------------------------|----------------|--------------------|
| Daily allowance (per diem)   | INR 3,000      | INR 2,000          |
| Hotel (per night, G1 to G3)  | INR 7,000      | INR 4,500          |
| Hotel (per night, G4 and G5) | INR 12,000     | INR 8,000          |

Air travel is permitted in economy class only for flights under 4 hours.

## 4. Reimbursement

Approved claims are reimbursed to the salary account within 10 working days of approval.

## 5. Non-reimbursable Items

- [ ] · Alcohol and tobacco.

- [ ] · Traffic fines and penalties.

- [ ] · Personal entertainment and minibar charges.
