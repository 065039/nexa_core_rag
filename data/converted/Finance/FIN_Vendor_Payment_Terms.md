## Vendor Payment Terms

Version 2026.1, effective 01 February 2026. Owner: Finance Controller.

### 1. Standard Payment Terms

Vendor invoices are paid within 45 days of receipt of a valid invoice (Net 45).

### 2. Invoice Requirements

- Invoice must quote a valid Purchase Order (PO) number.
- Vendor GSTIN must match the vendor master record.
- Invoices without a PO number are returned to the vendor.

### 3. Bank Detail Verification

Before the first payment, Finance verifies vendor bank details using a cancelled cheque and a penny-drop test.

### 4. Advance Payments

Advance payments are capped at 20% of the PO value and require Finance Controller approval.
